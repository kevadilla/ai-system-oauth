Privacy policy

"Gmail Organizer (Personal)" is a private, single-user Google OAuth application. Its only user is its owner. It reads the owner's own Gmail messages (to label and watch for expected emails) and the owner's own Google Calendar (read-only, to surface upcoming events). Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. No data from any other person is collected. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the account owner (kevadilla@gmail.com).
